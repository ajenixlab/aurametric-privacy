AuraMetric Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 19, 2026

AuraMetric (“we”, “our”, “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the AuraMetric mobile application handles information.

1. Information We Collect

AuraMetric is designed to work primarily offline. Your expense records, books, shopping items, and app preferences are stored locally on your device.

2. Financial Data

AuraMetric does not connect to your bank account, credit card, UPI, or any financial institution. We do not access your transactions from external services.

3. Backup Files

If you use Backup or Restore features, backup files may be saved to a folder selected by you on your device. These backup files remain under your control. AuraMetric only accesses backup files, media, or folders explicitly selected by you for backup, restore, import, export, or sharing purposes. We do not upload your backup files to our servers.

4. Permissions

AuraMetric may request the following permissions only to provide app features requested by you:

  • Camera Permission – Used for scanning receipts and capturing expense-related images.
  • Microphone Permission – Used for optional voice input or voice-based expense entry features.
  • Files and Media Access – Used only when you choose to import, export, restore, create, or share backup files and receipt images. AuraMetric only accesses files, media, or folders explicitly selected by you.
  • Notification Permission – Used to send reminders, alerts, recurring expense notifications, and other important app-related notifications.

AuraMetric does not access these features without your permission and only uses them for app functionality initiated or requested by you.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data.

6. Internet Usage

AuraMetric can function without an internet connection for core features.

7. Data Security

Your data remains on your device unless you manually export or share backup files. Please keep your device secure.

8. Children’s Privacy

AuraMetric is not directed toward children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when needed. Updated versions will be posted on this page.

10. Disclaimer

AuraMetric is a personal finance and expense tracking tool intended for informational and organizational purposes only. The app does not provide financial, investment, tax, legal, or accounting advice. Users are solely responsible for decisions made based on information entered into the app. Please consult a qualified professional for financial or legal advice.
